RFM Analysis Engine

Recency, Frequency, Monetary segmentation of your customer base

📅
Recency (R)
How recently a customer made a purchase. Customers who bought recently are more likely to buy again. Scored 1–5 (5 = most recent).
🔄
Frequency (F)
How often a customer makes purchases. Frequent buyers show strong engagement and brand loyalty. Scored 1–5 (5 = most frequent).
💰
Monetary (M)
How much money a customer has spent. High-value customers contribute most to revenue. Scored 1–5 (5 = highest spend).

Cohort Analysis

Track customer retention across acquisition cohorts over time

What is Cohort Analysis?

Cohort analysis groups customers by their acquisition month and tracks what percentage return in subsequent months. Month 0 = 100% (acquisition month). Green cells indicate high retention; red cells indicate churn.
